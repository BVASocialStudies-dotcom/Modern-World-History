A Decision Game · Manchester, England · 1834

The Piecer's Shift

You are Tom, age ten, working your first weeks as a piecer in a Manchester cotton mill. Four times during your shift, you'll face a hard choice. Choose carefully — your decisions shape what happens to you next.

Round 1 of 4 6:00 AM — Shift Begins
Based on real conditions in English textile mills in the 1830s, during the early years of Britain's Factory Acts.

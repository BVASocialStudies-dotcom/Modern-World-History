      Non-Aligned Movement. Countries shaded dark green were members of the Non-Aligned Movement during the Cold War, which refused to formally join either the US or Soviet side. The movement was founded at Belgrade in 1961, so shading appears from the 1960s tab on. Members are based on the 1987 membership list, plus Burma (a founding member that left in 1979), Venezuela (joined 1989), and Namibia (represented by SWAPO before independence). The list is the 1987 one, so some countries shown had not joined yet in the 1960s.
